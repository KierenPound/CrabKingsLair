Privacy Policy

App: UnderCroft | Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UnderCroft ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information We Collect

To provide core app functionalities, we collect and process the following information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and authentication credentials used to secure your account.
  • User Vault Data: Personal records, files, or information you explicitly enter into the app. All user data is secured in cloud databases protected by Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Technical Data: Basic device information required for app operation, security, and authentication sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to:

  • Authenticate your identity and grant secure access to your account.
  • Store and retrieve your vault entries across your devices.
  • Respond to support requests or security inquiries.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is hosted securely using cloud infrastructure (Supabase) located in secure data centres. We implement strict access controls, data encryption in transit and at rest, and Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that only you can access your encrypted records.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks. Data is only shared with backend infrastructure providers (e.g., Supabase) strictly to host and serve the app functionality.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under UK GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or erase your information.

In-App Account Deletion:
You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app at any time by navigating to Settings > Delete Account.

It is at the end of the page. Deleting your account permanently removes your login credentials, user profile, and stored vault data from our primary databases immediately.
